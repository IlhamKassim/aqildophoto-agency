# 05 — Student browsing

**What to build:** A Student can browse Convocation Events, then see the Approved Photographers, Packages, and open Time Slots available for a chosen event — everything needed to decide who and what to book.

**Blocked by:** 03 — Package & Add-on listing, 04 — Photographer opt-in & Time Slot definition

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] A Student can list/browse Convocation Events
- [ ] Given a Convocation Event, a Student can see the Approved Photographers who have opted in, along with their Packages and Add-ons
- [ ] Given a Convocation Event and Photographer, a Student can see which Time Slots are currently open
- [ ] Photographers with Photographer Status other than Approved never appear in browse results
